# Financial, Technical & Market Competitive Analysis

## 1. Technical Performance Benchmarks
- **Insertion Loss:** <0.4 dB @ 6 GHz (vs. 1.5–2.5 dB in traditional PIN diode switches).
- **Switching Speed:** Sub-10µs mechanical settling time.
- **Power Handling:** 25W continuous RF power capacity.
- **Throughput Retention:** Sustains >85% bandwidth across 4+ hops via full-duplex UNII-5..8 cross-band isolation.

## 2. Unit Economics & Margins
- **Raw BOM Cost (@ 10k Units):** $26.57
- **Assembly, Testing & Warranty Reserve:** $5.00
- **Total Unit COGS:** $31.57
- **Target Commercial MSRP:** $129.99
- **Target Gross Margin:** **75.7%**
- **Break-Even Point:** ~1,850 units sold at MSRP (assuming $180,000 upfront R&D/tooling NRE).
